Our Story

Rooted in Tradition,
Crafted with Passion

Born from a deep reverence for Andhra and Telangana's culinary traditions, Aha Yemi Ruchulu began as a dream to preserve the authentic flavors passed down through generations of Telugu families in Visakhapatnam.

Every recipe carries ancestral wisdom — the precise tempering of mustard seeds, the perfect balance of tamarind and jaggery, and the slow-cooked depth of our signature gravies. We source only the finest local ingredients from Guntur's famed chili farms and the pristine waters of the Andhra coast.
